About me

I started in audit at KPMG, then spent seven years operating businesses in Delhi - turning around distressed hostel properties, scaling an organic-food brand and making it a household name for clean produce and the trusted partner for Blinkit and Zepto, taking a GenAI analytics platform to market, and building a US firm's India operations from scratch. Different industries, same job: find what's broken in the model, fix it, make the numbers move.

In college I co-founded our Enactus chapter. Working with farmers under the Raftaar scheme to promote sustainable farming, and setting up The Ugly Harvest project, was all part of my work at Earthy Tales - helping farmers across India earn more from their produce. Long term, I want to help democratize wealth management for the people the industry ignores.

Now: MBA candidate at USC Marshall, Dean's Merit Scholar, and VP of the Entrepreneurship & Venture Management Association.
